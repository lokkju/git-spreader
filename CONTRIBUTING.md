# Contributing

Contribute if you want. Open an issue or a pull request.

**Rights:** by submitting a contribution (code, docs, or other material), you
assign to the maintainer all rights in it, and license it to the maintainer for
any use without restriction or compensation. The maintainer accepts or rejects
contributions at their sole discretion. git-spreader is licensed under
[Polyform Shield 1.0.0](LICENSE.md) (source-available, not open source); your
contribution may be distributed under that or any other license the maintainer
chooses.

**Commits:** use [Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org)
(`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, …) — releases and the changelog are generated from
them.
